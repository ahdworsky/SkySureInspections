Regular aerial checkups can spot small problems before they become expensive repairs. Here’s what we look for on every flight:

- **Hail & wind impact** — lifted tabs, bruised shingles, missing granules  
- **Flashing & sealant** — around chimneys, skylights, pipe boots, and vents  
- **Gutter clues** — blocked downspouts and debris patterns that signal water issues  
- **Soft spots & sagging** — areas that warrant a closer on-the-roof evaluation
