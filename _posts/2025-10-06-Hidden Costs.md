# The Hidden Costs of Ignoring Your Roof

### 🏠 Protecting What Protects You
Your roof quietly absorbs every storm and season. When small issues go unnoticed, they become major financial headaches. A tiny crack, loose shingle, or clogged gutter can evolve into leaks, insulation damage, and structural decay.

> **Key idea:** Most expensive roof repairs begin as **one invisible issue**—the type a drone can see before you feel it.

---

### 💸 The True Cost of “Out of Sight, Out of Mind”
- **Water damage repairs:** \$2,000–\$8,000  
- **Full roof replacement:** \$12,000–\$20,000  
- **Mold remediation:** \$1,500–\$6,000  
- **Ceiling & drywall repair:** \$500–\$2,000  

These bills often start with:
- Broken or lifted **flashing** around chimneys/vents  
- Subtle **granule loss** from hail/wind  
- **Pooling** in low-slope areas and valleys  
- Hairline **shingle punctures** or fastener issues
